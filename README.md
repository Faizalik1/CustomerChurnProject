# 📌 Project Overview

Customer retention is one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses.

In this project, an end-to-end Machine Learning pipeline was developed to predict whether a customer is likely to churn based on customer demographics, subscription details, contract information, payment methods, and service usage.

The project combines SQL, Python, Machine Learning, and Business Analytics to simulate a real-world business scenario.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

The company is experiencing customer loss every month.

Business wants to know:

- Why are customers leaving?
- Which customers are at highest risk?
- Which factors influence churn?
- How can churn be reduced?

The objective is to build a predictive model that helps the business proactively retain customers before they leave.

# 📊 Business Insights

The analysis identified several important factors influencing customer churn.

Examples include:

- Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate.
- Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to leave.
- High monthly charges increase churn probability.
- Certain payment methods show higher churn.
- Long-term contract customers are more loyal.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the company should:

- Offer discounts to high-risk customers.
- Encourage long-term contracts.
- Improve customer support.
- Provide loyalty rewards.
- Target customers with high churn probability.
